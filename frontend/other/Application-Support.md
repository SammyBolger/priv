# Application Reference Architecture Support

This document defines support for the Application Reference Architecture template and how it's maintained.

## Template Maintenance

The Application Reference Architecture template is maintained by the Enterprise Architecture team with input from the Architecture Review Board.

Issues, questions, and feedback on the template itself (clarity, structure, missing sections, examples) should be reported via:
- **GitHub Issues:** Open an issue in the ai4ea repository
- **Email:** enterprise.architecture@entegris.com

Use pull requests for proposed improvements to the template structure, examples, or guidance.

## Application Owner Responsibilities

Organizations that adopt this Reference Architecture template are responsible for:

- **Sections 1–8 content:** Defining your application domain, business drivers, portfolio, and standards alignment
- **Approval & sign-off:** Obtaining stakeholder approval from domain owners, IT operations, finance, and compliance
- **Architecture decisions:** Documenting decisions in ADRs; maintaining records of deviations from standards
- **Updates & maintenance:** Keeping the RA current as your architecture evolves; retiring outdated content
- **Governance:** Ensuring all solutions built to this RA conform to defined principles, positions, and standards

## Architecture Team Support

The Enterprise Architecture team provides:

- **Template stewardship:** Maintaining clarity, alignment with guardrails, and best practices
- **Guardrail framework:** Supporting alignment with AI4EA Policies, Principles, Positions, Practices, and Standards
- **ADR guidance:** Reviewing architecture decision records for completeness and consistency
- **Architecture review:** Reviewing RAs for compliance with enterprise standards before approval
- **Escalation:** ARB review for high-risk or non-compliant architectures

## Getting Started

1. **Copy the template** from `other/RA-Template-Confluence.md`
2. **Read the instructions** in each section
3. **Fill in Sections 1–8** specific to your application domain
4. **Submit for review** to enterprise.architecture@entegris.com with stakeholder list
5. **Incorporate feedback** and resubmit
6. **Publish** once approved (Confluence, wiki, or team repo)
7. **Maintain** as your architecture evolves
