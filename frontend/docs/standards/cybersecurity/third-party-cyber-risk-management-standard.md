## 1. Purpose

This document identifies controls Entegris shall implement to manage supplier risk and comply with Entegris Cyber Security Supplier Risk Management Policy.

## 2. Scope

This standard applies globally to all Entegris sites, where Suppliers are engaged for delivering services, materials, and products, for the following categories:

This standard is intended to cover the people, processes, and technologies used in an effective supplier management operational strategy, and is applicable to all Entegris personnel, such as, Sourcing and Procurement team members, Business Sponsors, Cyber Security team, Enterprise Architecture and security architecture teams, Supplier Risk Managers, and other people who will be involved in engaging and managing suppliers for various services and/or delivery of products to Entegris.

## 3. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Business Sponsors**
  - Designated Entegris employees, who are responsible for sponsoring, engaging and managing suppliers for supporting their business operations.
  - Responsible for managing supplier's performance, supplier risk assessment, and to ensure all supplier related risks are mitigated.
- **Cybersecurity Risk Manager**
  - *No responsibilities listed in source.*
- **Supplier Risk Managers**
  - Document and execute Entegris' supplier risk management program and processes.
  - Identify and assess supplier risks.
  - Assist by identifying risk remediation or mitigations.
- **Supplier Workforce**
  - Identify potential risk exposures.
  - Work with Supplier Risk Managers to develop corrective action or risk mitigation plans.

## 6. Requirements

Entegris must ensure that each Supplier is operating with good engineering and cybersecurity practices so that Entegris can be more secure and operate our business with greater reliability.

### 6.1 Acquisition Strategies (where appropriate)

- i. Procurement shall only be sourced from approved suppliers
- ii. Employ Contract Tools and Techniques (where appropriate) to protect from:
  - a. unauthorized production,
  - b. theft,
  - c. tampering,
  - d. insertion of counterfeits,
  - e. insertion of malicious software or backdoors, and
  - f. poor development practices throughout the system development life cycle.
    - i. Supplier Binding Agreements

Supplier and third-party partner contracts shall be used to implement appropriate measures designed to meet the objectives of Entegris' cybersecurity program and Cyber Supplier Risk Management Plan.

### 6.2 Supplier risk assessment and contract requirements

- **6.2.1** All IT Suppliers shall be risk assessed prior to on-boarding.
- **6.2.2** Entegris stipulates the following requirements, descriptions, and acceptance criteria shall be contained within acquisition contracts for information systems, operational technologies, system components, or information services.
  - **6.2.2.1** Security functional requirements.
  - **6.2.2.2** Security strength requirements.
  - **6.2.2.3** Security assurance requirements.
  - **6.2.2.4** Security-related documentation requirements.
  - **6.2.2.5** Requirements for protecting security-related documentation.
  - **6.2.2.6** Description of the information system development environment and environment in which the system is intended to operate; and
  - **6.2.2.7** Acceptance criteria.
- **6.2.3** All Suppliers contracts must include 'Right-to-Audit' clause, allowing Entegris or its authorized agents to request for copies of their SOC 1 or SOC 2 reports, or 3rd party security assessment reports, and other policies or standards for Entegris to review,
- **6.2.4** Supplier contract should also have clause that allows Entegris or its authorized agents to conduct cybersecurity audits, either on-line or on-premises site visits.
- **6.2.5** Entegris Procurement and Sourcing team must conduct preliminary assessment of all prospective Suppliers to verify the Supplier's financial conditions, business operations stability, and reputation.
- **6.2.6** Upon the preliminary assessment, Entegris Cybersecurity team must send out a Supplier Risk Assessment questionnaire specific to the services/products being procured, to all short-listed Suppliers.
- **6.2.7** Cybersecurity team shall prepare Risk Assessment report on each of the suppliers, based on their response to the assessment questionnaire.
- **6.2.8** Results of this risk assessment should be taken into consideration in final supplier selection decision

### 6.3 Technology Supplier's solution review

- **6.3.1** Entegris must ensure that each Supplier is operating with good engineering and cybersecurity practices so that Entegris can be more secure and operate our business with greater reliability
- **6.3.2** The business sponsor must present the technical solution proposed by the Supplier to Entegris Technology Review Board (TRB), and Architectural Review Board (ARB), to ensure that the proposed solution meets Entegris architectural standards, and meets cybersecurity and compliance requirements
- **6.3.3** Business sponsor must proceed with the engagement of the Supplier, only if the proposed solution meets the ARB guidelines.
- **6.3.4** Where the supplier's solution does not meet all Entegris technology and security requirements, an exception maybe considered, depending on business needs and nature of the gaps and risks identified.
- **6.3.5** Where Business Sponsor decides to engage a supplier with known risks and technology gaps, the business sponsor must discuss with suppliers to close known prioritized gaps and risks identified
- **6.3.6** Business Sponsor shall be responsible to monitor and report on the Supplier's risk mitigation activities progress

### 6.4 Supplier Risk mitigation

- **6.4.1** Once a supplier is confirmed, Business Sponsor must discuss with the Supplier to close known prioritized gaps identified in evaluation, immediately prior to start of the service engagement, or develop a plan to mitigate the identified risks in a timely manner.
- **6.4.2** Where it is necessary to start the engagement without mitigating the identified risks, the Business Sponsor must submit an Exception Request, acknowledging the risk involved and accepting the risk.
- **6.4.3** All suppliers' risks must be tracked and reported, until remediation or disposition, in Entegris GRC tool.

### 6.5 Supplier Risk levels definitions

- **6.5.1** Entegris should use multiple risk factors to identify a given Supplier's impact to the organization. Based on this assessment, Entegris shall classify its suppliers to different groups, as given below:
  - **6.5.1.1 Tier 1 Supplier** — These are business defined suppliers that pertain critical risk in providing business services. These should be defined by the sourcing organization.
  - **6.5.1.2 High Risk Supplier** — These include Tier 1 suppliers identified by sourcing and any additional High Risk suppliers that business sponsor may identify as critical.
  - **6.5.1.3 For IT and digital purposes, High Risk suppliers include the following:** Suppliers with -
    - Privileged administrative access for a High Risk system or application
    - Access to confidential / restricted data
    - Access to proprietary source code
    - Remote access to Entegris devices used in manufacturing or engineering or R&D
  - **6.5.1.4 Non-Critical Supplier** — All suppliers that are not Tier 1 or High Risk category will be considered as non-critical Suppliers.

### 6.6 Supplier Risk re-assessment

Entegris must ensure that each Supplier is operating with good engineering and cybersecurity practices, throughout their service delivery to Entegris. To ensure this, Entegris shall establish a process to re-assess the risk associated with its suppliers on periodic basis. Supplier risk assessments shall be conducted when there are significant changes to the supplier service contract, or when changes to the technology, system, service, environments of operation occur.

#### 6.6.1 Re-Assessments

Entegris requires periodic re-assessments of suppliers and third-party partners using audits, test results, or other forms of evaluations to confirm contract compliance, in accordance with cadence given below:

| Category | Tier | Cadence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All Suppliers (Default) | Tier 1 | Bi-annual |
| | Tier 2 | 3 years |
| | Tier 3 | 3 years |
| IT & Telecom | Tier 1 | Annual |
| | Tier 2 | Bi-annual |
| | Tier 3 | 3 years |

## 7. Supplier Risks Reporting and Notifications

Entegris may need to report certain risks and security/privacy related incidents to regulators and/or its customers, due to legal and contractual obligations.

- **7.1** All Entegris suppliers must report any cybersecurity related incidents or risks that are related to their operations that impact their services/production and/or delivery of goods to Entegris, as soon as possible, or in any case within 24 to 48 hours.
  - **7.1.1** Reporting and notification timelines shall depend on severity of the incident types, which needs to be established between Entegris Business Sponsor, Business Risk Manager, and the Supplier depending on the nature and criticality of the products/services to be delivered.
- **7.2** Business Sponsors or designated Business Risk Manager should be monitoring their supplier's performance and monitor the identified risks, threats and possible cyber attacks, and report on it periodically, and where necessary initiate corrective actions.

## 9. Exceptions Management

<!-- Standard section: do not edit. -->

1. Exceptions must be documented and reviewed with relevant IT and Business leaders.
2. All exceptions must be documented and submitted by the designated Business Leader stating the compelling reasons as to why the requirements cannot be met, and what other compensating controls are there to mitigate the risk to Entegris.
3. Any exception must be given for a limited period, a maximum of 1 year. Approved exceptions must be shared with the Cybersecurity team.

## 10. Standard Training & Distribution

All people resources shall be made aware of basic risk management principles. Making them aware of potential risk, how to identify and report potential risks.

Personnel responsible for conducting supplier risk assessments or audits should be advanced trained regarding Entegris' supplier risk management strategy and supporting processes.

Supplier Risk Assessments or Audits (SRA):
