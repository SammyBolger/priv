# Fast Follower on Exponential Technology

**Principle Name:** Fast Follower on Exponential Technology

**Statement:** Entegris adopts proven exponential technologies as a disciplined fast follower — moving quickly once enterprise value is demonstrated, rather than pioneering unproven technology at scale.

**Rationale:** First-mover adoption of emerging technology carries high risk and distraction cost. Fast-follower adoption captures the value without the experimentation overhead, aligned to Entegris's pragmatic, value-driven technology posture.

**Implications:**

- Technology scanning should focus on identifying proven enterprise value patterns in adjacent industries.
- Adoption decisions should weigh time-to-value, implementation risk, and organizational readiness.
- Pilots should validate fit for Entegris's specific context before scaled rollout is approved.
