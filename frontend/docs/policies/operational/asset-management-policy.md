## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish Asset Management procedures and practice to ensure all Entegris Technology systems related assets are inventoried and maintained through their lifecycle, and all unauthorized assets are investigated, and appropriate action taken, either to record the asset in the Asset Inventory or removed from our environment.

Having asset inventory is essential to manage our risks and secure our environment and strengthen our cybersecurity posture.

## 2. Scope

- This is an Entegris Global Policy
- Scope of Policy includes Entegris Technology Assets, listed below:
  - a. Servers
  - b. PCs and Laptops
  - c. PLCs
  - d. Applications
  - e. Databases
  - f. Mobile Devices (Mobile phones and tablets, etc.)
  - g. Any other OT assets, as determined by the business

## 3. Roles & Responsibilities

**Chief Information Officer (CIO)**

- Ensure Asset Manage programs, tools and processes are established, operational and effective
- Follow up with Business Leaders to ensure that all their assets are identified, inventoried, and tracked [is this the responsibility of IT or the business to inventory the assets?]
- Communicate critical/high risks and overall risk management posture to executive leadership

**Cyber Security Council**

The Cyber Security Council is a cross-functional technology and business leadership forum responsible to:

- Ensure that the technology teams are working on appropriately prioritized security initiatives and in line with business requirements
- Assign resources to the teams to remediate identified risks

**Business Leaders**

- Ensure that all assets under their areas of operations are identified and inventoried in the central asset register.
- Ensure that all required assets are procured through Procurement
- Ensure all their assets are maintained per established guidelines or per vendor instructions
- Ensure that each asset has an Entegris Employee assigned as Asset Owner

**Asset Owners (split this between Business and Technical)**

- Responsible to ensure that assets they own are registered in the central asset inventory register.
- Ensure that all required data about the asset are captured in the inventory.
- Ensure that the asset inventory is reviewed and updated, whenever changes made to the asset, or at least once annually.
- Ensure that assets are maintained per established guidelines or per vendor instructions
- [Do we want to ensure the assets have a relationship to the application?]

**Infrastructure & Operations Leads**

- Responsible for operation and maintenance of all IT systems
- Ensure maintenance activities, including patch management, are performed on all IT assets.
- Assign & manage designated resources for infrastructure maintenance and operations

**IT Service Delivery & Support team**

- Establish and operationalize Assent Inventory Register and related processes.
- Establish automated alerts and send communications to asset owners to ensure assets’ data is updated periodically and kept up to date.

**OT Teams**

- Responsible for operation and maintenance of OT systems
- Ensure maintenance activities, including patch management, are performed on all OT assets.
- Assign & manage designated resources for infrastructure maintenance and operations

## 6. Policy Requirements

### 6.1 Asset Management practices

- **6.1.1** Entegris shall establish policies, standards, procedures, and tools to manage all its Technology Systems assets, and to ensure no unauthorized assets are implemented or connected to Entegris networks.
- **6.1.2** Entegris shall establish procedures to ensure that Technology Systems assets are procured by authorized personnel through Procurement process, and with prior approval.

### 6.2 Assets Inventory

- **6.2.1** Entegris shall implement processes, and tools establish central asset registry to capture inventory of all technology systems related assets.
- **6.2.2** Physical assets and systems within the organization are all inventoried. All physical assets associated with Entegris Technology Systems, shall be identified, inventoried, and managed over the assets’ lifecycle.
- **6.2.3** Software platforms and applications within the organization are inventoried. All software assets associated with Entegris Technology Systems must be identified, inventoried, and must be managed over the assets’ lifecycle.
- **6.2.4** Asset inventory must be reviewed on a periodic basis to ensure accuracy, completeness.

### 6.3 Ownership of Assets

- **6.3.1** All assets must have designated owners [are we going to designate business and technology owners?]. The ownership must be assigned when assets procured, and recorded in Asset Registry
- **6.3.2** The asset owner shall be responsible for the effective management, and maintenance of the asset over its lifecycle.
  - **6.3.2.1** Each asset should also have a designated Technical Owner, who will be responsible for asset maintenance and operational performance of the asset

### 6.4 Asset Maintenance

- **6.4.1** Entegris Asset Owners shall be responsible for maintaining all the assets/systems, assigned to them.
- **6.4.2** Entegris must establish a process for maintenance personnel authorization and maintain a list of authorized maintenance organizations or personnel.
- **6.4.3** Entegris shall establish controls on the tools, techniques, mechanisms, and personnel used to conduct system maintenance.

### 6.5 Acceptable Use of Assets

- **6.5.1** All Entegris Technology systems must be used for its intended purpose only, in accordance with Entegris Acceptable Use Policy.

### 6.6 Return of Assets

- **6.6.1** Entegris employees, contractors, and other 3rd parties who are given Entegris technology assets for effective delivery of their work or service to Entegris, all such assets must be returned to Entegris on demand, or at the termination of their employment, contract, or agreement.
- **6.6.2** Entegris must establish a documented process to ensure that the return of assets is efficiently managed.

### 6.7 Asset Disposal

- **6.7.1** Entegris shall establish process to properly dispose all assets, at the end of their useful life or as determined by management.
- **6.7.2** Entegris must establish process to permanently delete all data from the asset’s hard drives/storage, prior to sending them for disposal.
- **6.7.3** A documented process must be established to ensure each asset is properly decommissioned and all data is transferred to designated storage (or destroyed permanently), and no residual data is left on assets hard-drive or in memory.

## 7. Exceptions Management

<!-- Standard section: do not edit. -->

- It is expected that all information technology and operational technology teams in Entegris will follow this policy requirements. Any exceptions to this policy, either partial or full, must be submitted by the designated Business/Technology Sponsor stating the compelling reasons as to why the requirements cannot be met, and what other compensating controls are there to mitigate the risk to Entegris.
- All exceptions must be given for a limited period, a maximum of 1 year.
- All exceptions must be documented per Entegris Exception Management Process.
