## Guidelines

It is essential that when traveling internationally on business purposes, particularly traveling to high-risk countries, all employees must consider the following guidelines to minimize data exposure and to protect Entegris confidential data:

1. Leave regular corporate devices back at home or office and obtain a loaner device.
2. Loaner devices shall have only limited number of applications and no data stored in local drives, and with USB ports disabled.
3. To the extent possible, access company email only through webmail.
4. To the extent possible, access all required applications for work through cloud.
5. Company portal application (Microsoft Intune) must be uninstalled from the mobile phone or iPad/tablets, prior to the destination country, so that no Entegris data can be accessed or downloaded to the phone.
6. Do not download any email attachment to the loaner device.
7. USB access should be disabled for all non-Entegris approved USB devices.
8. Do not use wi-fi networks available in public places, such as, airports or coffee shops and restaurants.

## Loaner Device

To protect Entegris proprietary information, intellectual property, and other sensitive / confidential data all employees traveling internationally where data encrypted devices are not permitted (or subject to forfeiture. Etc,.) for business purposes, must use a loaner device (laptop) which should be using during the duration of their travel.

This will also help Entegris meet country specific regulations on device encryptions, and protect Entegris data.

- Who should be requesting for a Loaner laptop?
  - Any Entegris employees or contractors traveling internationally from the home-office to other countries for business purposes, where data encrypted devices are not permitted
- How to get a Loaner device?
  - You should submit a request for loaner device, at least 1 week, prior to the date of travel
  - Loaner Device request must be submitted through ServiceNow

NOTE: All Loaner devices must be returned to the location, where it is issued from.

## Loaner Device Rules

**Smartphones/IPad:**

- Access email only through the Microsoft Outlook App (Intune Company Portal container)
- Access browser through the Microsoft Secure Browser (Intune Company Portal container)

**Do's:**

- As far as possible carry only loaner laptop devices when travelling
- Check email from loaner laptop browser or Microsoft Secure App on Smartphones/ IPads
- Use SharePoint and OneDrive for sharing and collaboration.
- Be careful and limit connecting to Free-WiFi networks in cafes/airports
- Be careful of non-work related website browsing from loaner devices

**Dont's:**

- Store any corporate data on personal devices
- Download any corporate email attachments to loaner laptop
- Tamper with Entegris apps settings on smartphone device

## Device swap process for critical users

### Before Travel

Critical List employee wants to travel to China.

- Decide on devices needed during travel
- Visit closest "Key Site" to collect loaner devices
- Review rules to handle loaner devices
- Leave regular corporate devices back

### During Travel

Travel to China with Loaner Devices ONLY.

- Adhere to defined rules while using loaner devices

### After Travel

Return back to Home Country.

- Return Loaner devices at "Key Site" Locations
- IT will:
  - Wipe-off all data off returned loaner devices
  - Avail loaner devices for future travelers
