## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the requirements and criteria for seeking exceptions to any Entegris information technology, operational technology, digital assets related architectural and cybersecurity controls, essential to protect Entegris Information and Data assets, and for securing Entegris operations. This policy:

- ensures that all control exceptions are risk assessed, approved, recorded, tracked, and reported
- helps businesses to focus on control gaps and address them prioritized based on risk severity
- Helps keep track of exceptions taken and overall risk posture
- Reduces overall risk, by reducing number of controls exceptions over a period

## 2. Scope

This Exception Policy is applicable to all IT and OT security and architectural controls as documented in various policies and standards that are applicable to information and data assets, operational technology assets.

This policy applies to all controls exceptions applicable to information and data assets, operational technology assets, information resources and related infrastructure or applications owned, managed, or administered by Entegris or owned and operated by a third-party on behalf of Entegris.

This policy is applicable to all Entegris personal responsible to implement controls and/or enforce compliance to controls, including business leaders, application owners, systems owners, infrastructure teams, OT teams, subject matter experts (SMEs), applications and systems architects, and cybersecurity team.

## 3. Roles & Responsibilities

**Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)**

- Ensure Exception Management program is operational and effective
- Follows-up with Business Leaders to ensure controls are implemented and risks are mitigated in a timely manner
- Communicate exceptions related risks and overall exception management posture to executive leadership

**Cyber Security Council**

The Cyber Security Council is a cross-functional technology and business leadership forum responsible to:

- Ensure that the exceptions management process is operational and adhered to by all
- Review Exceptions related risks and trends on periodic basis, and determine strategic actions to limit number of exception requests

**Cyber Security Risk Manager**

- Responsible for facilitating and operating the Exceptions Management process
- Establish process to review and communicate exceptions related risks and trends
- Monitor and report on approved exceptions and track them for resolving

**Policy Owners**

- Educate on policy control requirements, and on alternative controls, and help businesses to raise exceptions based on business justification
- Review Exception requests and provide guidance on remediation or mitigating controls
- Assess the risk associated with each exception and alert the Business leaders
- Where necessary, escalate unreasonable Exception requests or exceptions leading to high/severe risks to Cyber Security Council for resolution

**Business Leaders**

- Responsible for ensuring all cyber security controls are implemented for all assets and processes in their scope of operations,
- Review the need for taking an exception, and approve the exception requests accepting the associated risks
- Provide necessary resources to mitigate the exceptions, within the exception period

**Application / Systems Owners**

- Responsible to ensure the application/ system meets all applicable controls
- Identifies control gaps within their application/system process and initiates exception submission process

**Application/ Systems SME/ Tech Support Team**

- Identifies control gaps and informs Application Owner
- Provides input on how best to remediate the control gaps,
- Suggest compensating controls, incase, an exception is required
- Help Application/ systems Owner to submit an Exception Request
- Leads implementation of controls to mitigate gaps identified

## 6. Policy Requirements

### 6.1 Exceptions Management policy

- Entegris technology leadership must establish an Exception Management policy, and procedures, and tools for submitting technology exceptions for review, approval, and tracking
- Establish process for identifying control gaps and determine where controls exceptions are required. (Refer to Figure #1 Exception Management Process)

### 6.2 Criteria for seeking exceptions

In general, exceptions maybe considered under the following situations:

- The Business needs time to implement the required control and agreeing to complete the implementation within the exception period.
- The system/application under consideration is due for retirement or will be replaced within 6 – 12 months period
- A required control cannot be implemented due to technical limitations within the system/application
- Other unique business situations and/or operational conditions prevent Business to implement required security controls

NOTE: For all these above situations, it is expected that alternative mitigating controls will be implemented during the exception period or till the control requirements are met.

- All exception requests must be submitted, following process flow depicted in figure #1, and described in this and other sections of this policy.
- Exception requirements shall be determined based on the control gaps identified, per section 6.4 Control Gaps Identification and Risk Assessment, given below.
- All exception requests must be submitted, following the steps given in section 6.5
- Any exception should be given for a maximum period of 1 year. If further extension required, then a new exception request must be initiated and approved, per the process.

### 6.4 Control gaps identification and Risk Assessment

As Cyber threats keep increasing and cyber-attacks are getting more sophisticated, it is imperative that additional security controls will be needed to protect Entegris data on its applications and systems. Also, Entegris may need to update its control policies due to change in applicable regulations or sometime due to specific requirements from our customer agreements. All these factors require Entegris to update its current cybersecurity policies or create new policies and standard, to enforce additional controls in its technology environment.

At Entegris, exception process starts upon identifying control gaps, based on specific control requirements applicable to a particular application or system. Control gap identification will be done per the guidance given below:

#### 6.4.1 New applications and/or systems

- All new applications and/or systems will be subjected to Technology Review Board (TRB) and Architectural Review Board (ARB) review processes, and as part of this review, all required control will be identified based on the type of data the application/system is expected to collect, process, store, or transfer. Based on this analysis, if any control gaps are anticipated, an exception request must be initiated, prior to the production launch of the application/system.
- All exception requests must meet the Exceptions Criteria given in section 6.2, above.

#### 6.4.2 Existing applications and/or systems

- Existing application / systems may need an exception, due to release of new or updated Entegris policies or due to identification of control gaps through audits or other risk assessment processes.
- Whenever a new control policy issued or an existing policy is updated, or a control gap is identified, the application and/or system owner should conduct a gap analysis to confirm whether the application/systems meet the new control requirements or not. Where necessary, then an exception would be considered.
- All exception requests must meet the Exceptions Criteria given in section 6.2, above.

### 6.5 Exception submission

- Exception requests must be submitted through a formal process by the concerned business/technology lead.
  - All exception requests must go through a risk review process and risk assessed
  - Exception request shall also be reviewed by the concerned Policy Owner (the policy controls against which the exception is sought), work with the business/technology leads to conduct exception risk analysis and appraise the business /Technology VP on the potential risk to Entegris.
  - The Policy Owner may suggest alternative methods to meet the control intent, instead of seeking an exception.
- Every exception request must clearly state the justification for seeking the exception, and what compensating controls are in place to reduce the risk to Entegris, during the period of exception.
- All exception requests shall be approved by respective Technology VP and Business VP, acknowledging the risk associated with that specific exception
- The Policy Owner may escalate the exception request to the Cybersecurity Council, if the exception request is not meeting the exception request criterion.

### 6.6 Exception Validity

- All approved exceptions shall be valid for a maximum period of 12 months ONLY. The validity also depends upon the effectiveness of the counter measures implemented.
  - The Business shall take appropriate actions to fix the control gaps within the given exception period
  - Exception should be re-assessed prior to completion of exception validity period
- Where necessary, a new exception request must be submitted prior to the expiration of initial approval, and must again go through risk review & risk assessment, and approval process

### 6.7 Exceptions Tracking & Reporting

- Cybersecurity Policies and Risk Manager shall be responsible for establishing a process to record and track all exception requests, and their disposition
- Exceptions approved and the resulting risk to Entegris must be documented and reported to the ELT on a periodic basis
- Exceptions trends should be tracked, and suitable strategy shall be recommended to increase compliance and limit number of exceptions.

## 7. Exceptions Management

- It is expected that all information technology and operational technology teams in Entegris will follow this policy requirements. Any exceptions to this policy, either partial or full, must be submitted by the designated Business/Technology Sponsor stating the compelling reasons as to why the requirements cannot be met, and what other compensating controls are there to mitigate the risk to Entegris.
- All exceptions must be given for a limited period, a maximum of 1 year.
- All exceptions must be documented per Entegris Exception Management Process.

## 8. Policy Distribution and Training

- All security related policies must be clearly communicated to all employees and third parties. IT and OT Management will coordinate with Human Resources as needed to determine appropriate new employee orientation, security training and awareness content areas. All Suppliers and contractors shall be required to abide by these security related policies.

## 9. Policy Enforcement

- All security related policies will be enforced using either system automated or manual controls. All users are expected to comply with these policies and promote secure computing practices. Any user found to have violated this policy may be subject to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment and legal prosecution.
