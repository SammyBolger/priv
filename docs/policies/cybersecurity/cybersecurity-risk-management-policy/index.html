1. Purpose​

This policy provides guidance and make Entegris a risk managed organization by establishing requirements to develop a formal risk management program for identifying and managing cybersecurity risks associated with Entegris technology systems and components.

The objectives of this policy are to:

  • Ensure that all the current and future material cybersecurity related risks are identified, assessed, quantified, appropriately mitigated, and managed.
  • Develop and implement a sustainable cybersecurity risk management framework, processes.
  • Enable compliance with applicable regulations through the adoption of best practices.
  • Minimize technology related risks to personnel safety, process safety and security of all Entegris personnel and operating facilities.
  • Promote a pro-active approach in risk identification, reporting, evaluating, and resolving risks associated with the company’s operating asset.

2. Scope​

This policy applies to all information and data assets, information resources and related infrastructure owned, managed, or administered by Entegris or owned and operated by a third-party on behalf of Entegris.

This policy applies to anyone with access to Entegris information and information technology assets, including permanent, temporary, or contracted employees, consultants, or third-party organizations (henceforth referred to as the “users”).

This policy applies to engineering, maintenance, operations, lab, Research and Development, information technology (IT), and business units directly involved in or related to plant manufacturing and operations.

This policy is applicable to all Entegris Technology Systems, as defined in Section 4.0

3. Roles & Responsibilities​

Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) — Accountable (A)

  • Ensure Cybersecurity Risk Management programs, tools and processes are established, and are operational and effective.
  • Follow up with Business Leaders to ensure risks are identified and mitigated in a timely manner.
  • Communicate critical/high risks and overall risk management posture to executive leadership

Cyber Security Council — Accountable (A)

The Cyber Security Council is a cross-functional technology and business leadership forum responsible to:

  • Ensure that the technology teams are working on appropriately prioritized security initiatives and in line with business requirements.
  • Assign resources to the teams to remediate identified risks

Enterprise Architecture team — Responsible (R)

  • Provide architectural solutions that help mitigate or minimize technology related risks.
  • Review new products or Suppliers’ solutions to identify risks and advise business leaders and Cybersecurity team

Enterprise Risk Management — Accountable (A)

  • Communicate overall Enterprise risk appetite.
  • Establish guidelines for setting up risk tolerance limits.
  • Periodically report critical and high risks to ELT (Executive Leadership Team) and Board of Directors

Business Leaders — Responsible (R)

  • Responsible for ensuring all cyber security controls are implemented for all assets and processes in their scope of operations,
  • Review the need for taking an exception, and approve the exception requests
  • Responsible to ensure adequate resources are in place to implement and maintain requirements.

Operations Site Leader — Responsible (R)

  • Accountable to ensure all operations risks are mitigated

Cybersecurity Team (Risk management team) — Responsible (R)

  • Maintain the risk portfolio.
  • Establish risk impact assessment and prioritization guidelines.
  • Establish process to communicate risks to the impacted asset owners, and monitor & report on remediation status

Infrastructure & Operations team — Responsible (R)

  • Identify emerging risks to the infrastructure environment and asses their impacts.
  • Take remediation actions on identified risks

MOT — Responsible (R)

  • Ensures the divisions and plants are closely aligned on the technology and security strategy.
  • Drive operational standards to reduce cybersecurity and operational risk.
  • Assist business leaders and technologists in understanding Operational Technology risks and ways to manage them effectively.
  • Identify key trends across Operational Technology environments to thwart emerging threats and vulnerabilities

Supplier Risk Manager — Responsible (R)

  • Conduct Supplier’s risk assessments and identify supplier risks and follow-up on mitigation. Ensure that:
    • Suppliers are participating and supporting Entegris cyber risk management requirements.
    • Suppliers are identifying risks within their environment and inform Entegris in a timely manner.
    • Periodically update Entegris on supplier’s risk mitigation strategies and progress made

6. Policy Requirements​

This policy shall provide risk management guidance for the Entegris Technology Systems. This policy shall be supported by suitable Risk Management Standards, procedures, and tools. The risk management has three distinct phases: Risk Management, Risk Assessment, and Risk Mitigation

6.1 Comprehensive cyber risk management program​

Entegris shall establish a comprehensive cyber risk management program, that includes developing and publishing Risk Management policy and standard, and work processes, to:

  • 6.1.1 Identify – Develop an organizational understanding to manage cybersecurity risk to systems, people, assets, data, and capabilities.
  • 6.1.2 Protect – Develop and implement appropriate safeguards to ensure delivery of critical services.
  • 6.1.3 Detect – Develop and implement appropriate activities to identify the occurrence of a cybersecurity risk event.
  • 6.1.4 Respond – Develop and implement appropriate activities to take action regarding a detected cybersecurity risk incident.
  • 6.1.5 Recover – Develop and implement appropriate activities to maintain plans for resilience and to restore any capabilities or services that were impaired due to a cybersecurity incident.
  • 6.1.6 Manage and mitigate risks from Suppliers and Third-parties or partners.

6.2 Risk management tools​

Entegris should also implement tools to conduct effective risk management activities, as given in sections 6.1.

6.3 Risk impact assessment and prioritization​

  • 6.3.1 Entegris should establish process to conduct risk impact assessments and then prioritize risks for further action.
  • 6.3.2 Entegris shall use the Risk Matrix given below, as a guide to assess the impacts of a specific risk.

  • 6.3.2.1 Business divisions must establish risk tolerance limits. With an understanding of risk tolerance, Business Divisions should prioritize cybersecurity activities, enabling business to make informed decisions about cybersecurity expenditures.
  • 6.3.2.2 While aggregating all risks to Entegris overall risk posture, risk financial impacts should be calculated at Business Division level, applying weightage based on that business’s contributions to overall Entegris revenue.

6.4 Managing Risks and reporting​

  • 6.4.1 Each Business Division shall designate person/s to manage their division’s cyber risk activities.
  • 6.4.2 Each risk must have a designated Risk Owner, and the Risk Owner shall be responsible to arrange resources to mitigate the risks.
  • 6.4.3 Entegris shall establish a central risk register and track all risks, and ensure all risks are addressed and responded to in a timely manner. All risks must be categorized, and aligned to business processes and sub-processes, as applicable.
  • 6.4.4 Entegris shall establish process to periodically update Executive Leadership members on all critical and high risks.

6.5 Risk response planning & monitoring​

  • 6.5.1 All risks should be recorded in a central risk register and must be assigned a Risk Owner
  • 6.5.2 Entegris shall establish process to assess risk impacts and initiate appropriate response based on risk severity, likelihood of occurrence, threat environments, and business conditions. The risk response options may include, to mitigate risk, transfer risk, or accept risk.
  • 6.5.3 All risks in Risk Register shall be reviewed periodically for continued relevance, vis-à-vis changes in the business operational conditions or threat environment and re-assess the risk impacts and relevancy of the risk to the organization. The review frequency shall be determined based on risk level, but in any case, not more than one year.
  • 6.5.4 Where it is decided to mitigate a risk, the mitigation progress must be tracked and reported until the mitigation is completed, and the risk no longer applicable.
  • 6.5.5 Where Business division decides to accept a risk, an exception must be submitted, and shall be approved by the Business Leader

7. Risk Management Training​

  • 7.1.1 Entegris will develop role-based Cyber risks management training program for all the stakeholders involved in the risk management activities.
  • 7.1.2 The training materials should be reviewed on periodic basis, and make necessary updates, and re-train the stakeholders.

8. Exceptions Management​

  • It is expected that all Entegris technology teams will follow this policy requirements.
  • Any exceptions to this policy, either partial or full, must be submitted by the designated Business / Technology Sponsor stating the compelling reasons as to why the requirements cannot be met, and what other compensating controls are there to mitigate the risk to Entegris.
  • All exceptions must be given for a limited period, a maximum of 1 year.
  • All exceptions must be documented per Entegris Exception Management Process.

9. Policy Distribution and Training​

  • All cybersecurity related policies must be clearly communicated to all employees and third parties.
  • Entegris Management will coordinate with Human Resources as needed to determine appropriate new employee orientation, security training and awareness content areas.
  • All Suppliers and contractors shall be required to abide by these security related policies.

10. Policy Enforcement​

All security related policies must be enforced using either system automated or manual controls. All users are expected to comply with these policies and promote secure computing practices. Any user found to have violated this policy may be subject to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment and legal prosecution.
