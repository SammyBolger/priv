1. Purpose​

This document describes the universal process. It is the Entegris Way.

This policy deals with managing Supplier cybersecurity risks associated with Entegris' suppliers, vendors, or other entities (collectively referred to as Suppliers) who provide materials, services, or products for Entegris to manage its product development, manufacturing, information systems, and operations.

This policy is essential because Entegris operations depend on these Suppliers, which allows us to succeed in the marketplace. To be successful, Entegris must ensure that all the risks associated with these Suppliers are assessed and mitigated.

Entegris must ensure that each Supplier is operating with industry standard engineering and cybersecurity practices so that Entegris can be more secure and operate our business with greater reliability.

Entegris may also need to demonstrate to our customers that Entegris has Supplier risk management practices in line with industry standards, which in turn help boost confidence in our operations management.

Managing Supplier risks essential for certain contract conditions, or for complying with regulatory requirements.

2. Scope​

2.1 This policy is intended to cover the people, processes, and technologies used in an effective supplier management operational strategy, and is applicable to all Entegris personnel, such as, Sourcing and Procurement team members, Business Sponsors, Cyber Security team, Enterprise Architecture and security architecture teams, Supplier Risk Managers, Quality teams and other people who will be involved in engaging and managing suppliers for various services and/or delivery of products to support Entegris Technology systems and operations.

2.2 This policy applies globally to all Entegris sites, where Suppliers are engaged for delivering services, materials, and products.

2.3 Out of scope: Entegris Customers with access to our systems or data.

3. Roles & Responsibilities​

The following is a list of stakeholders and their responsibilities for implementing and operating this policy. Accountable (A) / Responsible (R) is noted for each role.

Business Sponsor — Accountable (A)

  • Ensure supplier onboarding has followed policy needs and owns mitigation actions for known defects
  • Support the supplier evaluation process
  • Responsible for mitigating all risks related to their suppliers
  • Provide business justification for suppliers who do not meet required risk posture

Chief Information Security Officer — Accountable (A)

  • Ensure Supplier Risk Management program is operational
  • Follow-up with Business Leaders to ensure Supplier risks are mitigated in a timely manner
  • Communicate Supplier risks and overall supplier risk posture to Executive Leadership

Sourcing / Procurement Managers — Responsible (R)

  • Act as Entegris Point of Contact and liaison between the Business Sponsor, Supplier Risk Manager, Legal and other stakeholders, and with the potential suppliers
  • Manage the RFP process and short-list potential suppliers
  • Responsible to verify and ensure that a potential Supplier has good reputation and doing business with them will not impact reputation of Entegris
  • Work with Business Sponsor to understand business needs and help negotiate the contract

Cybersecurity Supplier Risk Manager (Cybersecurity Team) — Responsible (R)

  • Work with Business Sponsors, manage Supplier Risk Assessment activities, and follow-up on compliance gaps and ensuring gaps are closed or mitigated
  • Report on Supplier risk profiles, and overall Entegris Supplier Risk posture and compliance metrics
  • Plan periodic risk re-assessments of the identified Suppliers

Enterprise Architecture — Responsible (R)

  • Perform supplier assessment and architectural review for all technology suppliers' solutions identified

6. Policy Requirements​

6.1 Entegris must ensure that each Supplier is operating with good engineering and cybersecurity practices so that Entegris can be more secure and operate our business with greater reliability.

6.1.1 All Suppliers shall be risk assessed prior to on-boarding.

6.1.2 Business Sponsor must work with suppliers to close known prioritized gaps identified in evaluation in a timely manner and provide justification to Sourcing where suppliers do not meet right posture.

6.1.3 Supplier risk management roles and responsibilities related to supplier management activities shall be defined.

6.1.4 For all technology suppliers, Entegris should verify that the solution offered by the Supplier meets Entegris Architecture and secure architecture requirements, and the solution is supportable with people with appropriate skills, and other resources necessary to operate and maintain the solution.

6.1.4.1 Entegris Enterprise Architecture team must ensure all technology suppliers and their solution use has gone through a review process.

6.1.5 Suppliers providing direct support of Entegris systems and devices must have a Master Services Agreement in place. Access to Entegris networks and systems must not be granted unless such an agreement exists.

6.2 Supplier Risk levels definitions​

Entegris should use multiple risk factors to identify a given Supplier's impact to the organization. Based on this assessment, Entegris shall classify its suppliers, as given below:

6.2.1 Tier 1 Supplier — These are business defined suppliers that pertain critical risk in providing business services. These should be defined by the sourcing organization.

6.2.2 High Risk Supplier — These include Tier 1 suppliers identified by sourcing and any additional High-Risk suppliers that business sponsor may identify as critical.

6.2.2.1 For IT and digital purposes, High-Risk suppliers include the following. Suppliers with -

  • Privileged administrative access for a High-Risk system or application
  • Access to confidential / restricted data
  • Access to proprietary source code
  • Remote access to any Entegris devices

6.2.3 Non-Critical Supplier — All suppliers that are not Tier 1 or High-Risk category will be considered as non-critical Suppliers.

6.3 Supplier Risk Assessment​

Managing Supplier risks essential for certain contract conditions, or for complying with regulatory requirements.

6.3.1 Supplier contracts must include requirements related to secure business practices to be followed by the Supplier.

6.3.2 All Suppliers shall be classified into either Tier 1, High Risk or Non-critical suppliers, based on the products/services they provide to Entegris, and their potential impact on Entegris in the event of performance failure by the Supplier.

6.3.3 Tier 1 / High Risk Suppliers shall be periodically risk assessed, and corrective measures shall be taken, if any required, to mitigate identified risks.

6.3.4 All new and renewed Supplier contracts shall include a clause that, "Entegris (or its authorized agents) can gain access to Supplier's systems/documents, and facilities to conduct on-site audits, or virtual or online assessments, if necessary."

6.3.5 Entegris shall document its risk analysis of Suppliers and maintain the records.

6.4 Incident Notifications​

Entegris will notify its customers, in accordance with applicable regulations or contract requirements, should an adverse cyber event occur at one of our suppliers that will impact production or delivery of goods to the customer.

6.5 OT supplier control requirements​

6.5.1 Suppliers must be on Entegris' Qualified Supplier List.

6.5.2 Cybersecurity risks due to suppliers and other dependencies must be identified and addressed.

6.5.3 Supplier systems, assets, and software must be in compliance with the applicable Entegris policies, standards, and procedures.

6.5.4 The asset inventory, system architecture, access control, and vulnerability status of all third party owned / leased systems must be delivered and maintained by the supplier. Suppliers must maintain a security posture and comply with Entegris requirements throughout the engagement lifecycle.

6.5.5 Confidentiality agreements must be in place and up to date to comply with Entegris third party requirements.

6.5.6 All systems and software procured by supplier for use at Entegris must be approved through a change management process.

6.5.7 Supplier systems and software must be periodically reviewed.

6.5.8 When adding new software, only supplier-supported software shall be used (or approved open-source software).

6.5.9 New supplier software shall be added to Entegris software inventory.

7. Exceptions Management​

It is expected that all Entegris technology teams will follow this policy requirements.

  • Any exceptions to this policy, either partial or full, must be submitted by the designated Business / Technology Sponsor stating the compelling reasons as to why the requirements cannot be met, and what other compensating controls are there to mitigate the risk to Entegris.
  • All exceptions must be given for a limited period, a maximum of 1 year.
  • All exceptions must be documented per Entegris Exception Management Process.

8. Policy Distribution and Training​

  • All cybersecurity related policies must be clearly communicated to all employees and third parties.
  • Entegris Management will coordinate with Human Resources as needed to determine appropriate new employee orientation, security training and awareness content areas.
  • All Suppliers and contractors shall be required to abide by these security related policies.

9. Policy Enforcement​

  • All security related policies must be enforced using either system automated or manual controls.
  • All users are expected to comply with these policies and promote secure computing practices.
  • Any user found to have violated this policy may be subject to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment and legal prosecution.
