1. Purpose​

This policy governs the cybersecurity of all Entegris information technology (IT) assets and systems, including both Hardware and Software, across IT and operational technology (OT) environments.

This policy is required because technology assets and information systems deployed at Entegris contain confidential, proprietary, and competitive information which is critical to our manufacturing operations and overall success in the marketplace. These information systems and assets must be safeguarded and kept secure from both internal and external threats.

1.1 Background​

Entegris depends on its information systems for all its operations as they are the backbone for keeping a competitive edge in the market. Hence, security and risk management of all information systems is vital to our growth and continued viability as a successful business entity. Any attack on our systems can lead to loss of production, potentially lead to severe financial losses or reputational damage, and impact on our customer relations. Hence, it is imperative for us to ensure all Entegris technology assets and information systems are monitored and safeguarded from these attacks.

2. Scope​

The policy applies globally to all Entegris sites and facilities.

Cybersecurity is a shared responsibility. The involvement and participation of all Entegris employees, contractors and third-party service providers is essential to successfully implementing this policy. Hence this policy applies to all Entegris employees, contractors and service providers. This policy is applicable to all IT and OT assets and systems.

3. Roles & Responsibilities​

Chief Information Officer

  • Responsible for protecting all Entegris Information Technology assets and systems
  • Responsible for establishing Information Security policy and implementation of it
  • Establish information security organization and provide resources for its effective operation
  • Provide updates to Board of Directors on Entegris Cybersecurity posture, potential threats and risk mitigations

Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

  • Manage Cyber Security policy and ensure cybersecurity and risk management program is operational and effective
  • Establish and manage Cybersecurity Awareness and Education programs across the organization
  • Equip and enable Business Leaders to understand cyber security control requirements, alternative controls, and clearly identify areas of responsibility to manage their critical assets, and help businesses to raise policy exceptions based on business justification
  • Follows-up with Business Leaders to ensure cyber security controls are implemented and risks are mitigated in a timely manner
  • Communicate information security related risks and overall security posture to executive leadership
  • Review Exception requests coming from Business teams, if necessary, provide feedback or escalate.
  • Responsible for establishing and operating the cyber security and risk management policies and processes
  • Establish Exceptions management policies and process, and to review and communicate exceptions related security risks and trends
  • Ensure Entegris Risk Register is maintained and risk mitigation activities are tracked, and provide periodic updates to Cybersecurity Council and leadership

Cyber Security Council

The Cyber Security Council is a cross-functional technology and business leadership forum responsible to:

  • Ensure that the cyber security process is operational and adhered to by all
  • Review Exceptions related risks and trends on periodic basis, and determine strategic actions to limit number of exceptions requests

Business Leaders

  • Responsible for ensuring all cyber security controls are implemented for all assets and processes in their scope of operations,
  • Review the need for taking an exception, and approve the exception requests
  • Responsible to ensure adequate resources are in place to implement and maintain cybersecurity requirements.
  • Support all cybersecurity initiatives and communicate to their respective teams the importance of cybersecurity and adherence to security requirements to protect Entegris information systems

Entegris Global EHS team

  • Responsible for establishing physical security policies and procedures that support cybersecurity initiatives
  • Responsible for implementing physical security controls related devices and applications,
  • Manage day-to-day physical security operations and report security incidents, and
  • Resolve physical security related risks and issues

6. Policy Requirements​

As system and data systems custodians across Entegris, Information Technology will deploy cybersecurity policies and protocols globally, covering various security control domains, as listed below:

6.1 Asset Management​

Entegris must establish and implement an Asset Management policy that provides clear guidance on assets procurement, lifecycle management, and sunsetting of assets. In addition, the policy must define requirements on establishing a central asset management repository, and guidelines on asset classification and controls, and ongoing asset monitoring requirements.

6.2 Physical Security​

  • 6.2.1 Access to Entegris systems and networks – physical access to Entegris data centers, servers, networks and to our endpoint devices (inclusive of laptops and mobile devices) should be restricted to only authorized persons.
  • 6.2.2 Physical Access to Data Centers and System Servers – Entegris Site management in conjunction with Entegris Global EHS must ensure that only authorized users will have physical access to IT Data Centers-and network closets, and other critical file cabinets, etc.
  • 6.2.3 Physical Access for Endpoint Devices – Entegris Global EHS shall publish and communicate physical security guidelines to all Entegris users on how to protect the endpoint devices assigned to them.

6.3 Logical Access Security​

Access to Entegris data and applications must adhere to the following access management controls:

  • 6.3.1 Define and enforce policies that restrict access to systems and data based on roles, attributes, and contextual factors. Implementation and enforcement of access management policies that govern the identification, authentication, and authorization of users and systems to access organizational resources.
  • 6.3.2 Access to applications and other systems must be given through appropriate approvals and establishing baseline access controls.
  • 6.3.3 Controls such as, Account Management, Access Enforcement, Password standards, multi-factor authentication, Least Privilege, Remote Access, and Access Control for Mobile Devices must be established.
  • 6.3.4 Entegris must adopt Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) or Attribute Based Access Control (ABAC) models to assign permissions
  • 6.3.5 Audit and Accountability (AU) - Log access events and monitor for unauthorized access attempts.
  • 6.3.6 Ensure that access rights are reviewed periodically and updated, as necessary.
  • 6.3.7 Policy Enforcement and Decision Points - Implement Policy Enforcement Points (PEPs) and Policy Decision Points (PDPs) to evaluate and enforce access decisions dynamically.

6.4 Removeable Media / Cloud Storage Devices​

Entegris will provide standard, supported solutions for business related storage, sharing and collaboration of files. With those solutions in place, data should not be copied to USB drives from corporate issued PCs (laptops and desktops) or copy or forward to personal emails or personal public cloud storage.

6.5 Software Security​

All IT assets must be protected with anti-malware and antivirus software, installed and maintained by Global IT to safeguard against computer viruses and other forms of malicious code.

  • 6.5.1 Entegris Cybersecurity teams must develop and implement software vulnerabilities management policies, processes and tools.
  • 6.5.2 The Entegris Sourcing, Cybersecurity, and Global Service Desk must ensure that all software products procured from vendors are chosen following Entegris solution selection process, and are defect free and tested for vulnerabilities prior to installation in the Entegris Environment.
  • 6.5.3 Any software developed by Entegris, either in-house or by its 3rd parties, for use in Entegris products shall be developed using Secure Software Development practices and must be tested for vulnerabilities prior to shipping to Customers. To the extent possible, all custom-built software should use an Entegris controlled code repository and enforce version control processes.

6.6 Network Security​

Entegris shall develop a network security policy and implement it to protect Entegris networks from unauthorized access and ensure that the network infrastructure meets Entegris business needs.

Entegris shall develop network security architecture that protects its networks integrity by incorporating proper network segmentation, where appropriate, to segregate networks and limit network breach propagation. IT and OT networks will be separated and network traffic traversing between those environments and within the OT network will be highly controlled to protect the integrity of our plant systems.

6.7 Hardware Security and Systems hardening​

Entegris technology teams shall develop standards, procedures, tools and job aids to ensure IT and OT hardware systems and components meet Entegris Cybersecurity and operational requirements. These should include product selection criteria, vendor’s design/development practices, product verification & testing, and integration requirements.

Entegris shall also establish configuration management and hardening practices for the security of all the device/systems software through the supported product life.

6.8 Monitoring and Logging​

Access to Entegris technology systems and networks shall be monitored at all times and access logs shall be maintained. Any anomalies in individual access should be investigated. All access logs must be immutable and retained for a period consistent with data retention requirements.

6.9 Change Management​

All changes to the information systems and IT assets must be controlled and governed. Any proposed technology changes should go through a Change Control process, risk-assessment, and changes implemented in a controlled manner that limits disruption to business operations.

6.10 Incident Management and Response​

Entegris shall establish a comprehensive incident management and response program to ensure the timely identification, handling, and resolution of cybersecurity and related incidents. This program will aim to minimize disruption to Entegris technology environments and ensure compliance with regulatory breach reporting requirements.

  • 6.10.1 Security incidents must be clearly defined and prioritized based on severity to ensure appropriate visibility and timely mitigation. These incidents may include, but are not limited to, exposure of confidential data, criminal activity or investigations, denial-of-service attacks, malicious code, policy violations, unauthorized monitoring, rogue services, spam sourcing, unauthorized access, unpatched vulnerabilities, web defacement, or false alerts. The Security Incident Response Policy outlines the procedures for identifying, managing, and resolving such incidents.
  • 6.10.2 All mitigated major security related incidents need to be documented and reported to the appropriate senior management level. The incident escalation procedure ensures that management of incidents is based on priority and severity. Based on the criticality/severity of the security breach incident, Global IT will work with the Incident Response Team to communicate breach incident reports with regulatory bodies, as necessary.

6.11 Cybersecurity Risk Management​

Entegris should establish a comprehensive risk management program, that includes developing and publishing a Risk Management policy and standard. Entegris must also implement tools, where supported and feasible, to conduct effective risk management operations, including capturing of risks, impact assessments, mitigation, closure, risk monitoring and reporting.

6.11.1 Technology Supplier risk management​

  • Entegris should establish a comprehensive supplier risk management program, that includes developing and publishing a Supplier Risk Management policy and standard, and work processes to ensure all suppliers go through a preliminary vetting process, and then initial and periodic risk assessments conducted for all critical suppliers.
  • Entegris must also ensure that any suppliers’ access to Entegris information systems and networks is controlled and provided following a technology review and approval process.
  • Entegris must publish periodically critical supplier risk assessment reports for the review of Entegris ELT

6.12 Cybersecurity Governance​

Entegris shall establish and operationalize a comprehensive IT Governance structure and program to oversee governance activities and ensure the effective implementation of IT General Controls and other regulatory requirements. The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) will be accountable for conducting periodic control assessments, identifying gaps, and managing remediation programs to address those gaps.

6.13 Cybersecurity of new and emerging technologies​

Entegris shall develop and implement a formal process to evaluate the cybersecurity implications of new and emerging technologies considered for adoption. This process will include risk assessments to identify potential threats posed by such technologies and provide guidance to business leaders on secure adoption strategies and mitigation of identified risks. Where necessary, Cybersecurity team shall update existing cybersecurity policies and/or develop and publish new policies to help adopt new technologies for use at Entegris.

6.14 International Travel Cybersecurity​

It is essential that when traveling internationally on business purposes, all employees must follow Entegris International Travel Cybersecurity Guidelines and consider the destination country’s regulations and laws regarding technology being brought or imported. These steps are necessary to minimize Entegris data exposure, ensure regulatory compliance and to protect Entegris confidential and restricted data.

7. Exceptions Management​

  • Entegris must develop an Exceptions Management policy and establish a procedure to manage exceptions.
  • Exception must be submitted whenever any specific security policy requirement cannot be implemented or enforced due to technical or other operational reasons.
  • All exceptions must be documented, risk assessed, approved, and periodically reported for the review of Cybersecurity Council and ELT

8. Policy Distribution and Training​

  • All security related policies must be clearly communicated to all employees and third parties. Entegris IT Leadership will collaborate with Human Resources as needed to define appropriate content for new employee orientation, training and awareness. All Suppliers and contractors shall be required to abide by these security related policies.

9. Policy Enforcement​

  • All security related policies will be enforced through a combination of automated system or manual controls. All users are expected to comply with these policies and promote secure computing practices. Any user found to have violated this policy may be subject to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment and legal prosecution.
