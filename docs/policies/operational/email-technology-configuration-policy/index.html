1. Purpose​

1.1 Maintain an efficient and reliable email system and employ best practices in maintaining and storing current and dated information.

1.2 The policy helps employees understand requirements of Entegris email configuration.

2. Scope​

Entegris corporate email system globally.

3. Responsibilities​

3.1 All E-mail technical configuration is controlled globally by IT. End-users can change their email display settings but are strictly forbidden to tamper with application configuration settings that are centrally managed.

4. References​

  • Reference Documents: None listed.
  • Special Notes: Policy # BAI03.05.001

6. Policy​

6.1 User Classification:​

  • 6.1.1 All Entegris employees will utilize Microsoft Office 365 (O365) for all work-related e-mail messaging.
    • 6.1.1.1 Access to mail can be through the full client (Outlook) or browser-based Outlook Web App (OWA)

6.2 Office 365 Email Specifications:​

  • 6.2.1 O365 mailboxes will be accessed utilizing the O365 client and/or the Outlook Web App (OWA).
  • 6.2.2 O365 will have an active mailbox (inbox) size limit of 100GB.
  • 6.2.3 Mailbox Size (Send/ Receive) Limits:
    • 6.2.3.1 Users will be warned that they are approaching the storage limit of their inbox at 99GB and will be prohibited from sending at 99.5GB.
    • 6.2.3.2 Users will be unable to receive email when the mailbox reaches 100GB.
      • 6.2.3.2.1 Users can work with the Entegris Helpdesk to further archive/ purge email to get under the 100GB limit.
  • 6.2.4 Send and receive messages size (w/attachments) is limited to 25MB.
  • 6.2.5 Email less than 180 days old will be stored in the inbox.
  • 6.2.6 Email older than 180 days will get moved automatically to the employee’s archive mailbox.
    • 6.2.6.1 Users must be connected to Office 365 to access archived email; this means archived mail cannot be accessed if the user is not connected to the internet.
    • 6.2.6.2 When archived messages are 3 years old, they will be automatically deleted (3-Year retention policy). Users will not be able to access email older than 3 years. Any email older than two years will be deleted and will not be restorable from backup.
    • 6.2.6.3 The archive mailbox will have a default size of 100GB.
    • 6.2.6.4 Users need to work with the Entegris Helpdesk to preserve any business-critical email long term (> 3 years) in an appropriate document management system as mentioned above, not in email.
  • 6.2.7 User recoverability options:
    • 6.2.7.1 Users will be able to recover email from the deleted items folder for up to 30 days. After 30 days email will not be recoverable.
  • 6.2.8 Entegris does not allow the creation or retention of PST (Personal Storage File or Personal Folder Files stored locally on the PC).

6.3 Terminated Users​

If requested by HR or the Manager at the time of termination, Managers will have up to 30 days to review an employee’s email prior to permanent deletion. Email will not be forwarded. If Managers do not request access to the terminated employee’s mailbox, the 30-day deletion rule mentioned above will apply.

6.4 Legal Hold​

  • 6.4.1 Employees identified as being part of an Entegris legal hold will be informed of their compliance obligations related to legal holds, and IT will suspend the 2-year email retention policy until such time as the legal hold is terminated.
  • 6.4.2 Once the legal hold is removed, the 3-year email retention policy will be enforced and email older than 3 years will be automatically deleted.
  • 6.4.3 IT will work with Legal to perform a semi-annual review for all legal hold individuals.

6.5 Storage of Restricted Information:​

The email system should not be used to store restricted information, including iTAR controlled data. All data controlled by iTAR regulations should be stored on the approved corporate iTAR system/ application, where it is properly restricted and controlled.

6.6 Backup and Recovery:​

  • 6.6.1 Since email will be stored off site at Microsoft’s data center as a cloud provider, there is no internal Entegris email backup. Microsoft maintains their own backup and recovery environment only to ensure availability of the Office 365 application for its customers.
  • 6.6.2 Backups cannot be used for the recovery of historical email that was deleted and surpassed the 30-day soft delete period.
  • 6.6.3 User deleted email cannot be recovered beyond 30 days.

6.7 Shared Mailboxes:​

Shared mailboxes are used by global back-office teams like Customer Service for order taking, providing quotes, processing invoices, etc. All shared mailboxes have no archive policy. The general Inbox folder has a retention policy of 4 yrs. After 4yrs, any email from the Inbox folder will automatically be deleted.

6.8 Exception Handling:​

  • 6.8.1 In extremely rare circumstances, there could be instances where compliance and business requirements warrant the need for longer retention schedules (> 2 years). These exceptions will be handled individually and:
    • 6.8.1.1 Will be reviewed for appropriate business justification and evidence of laws mandating the request.
    • 6.8.1.2 Reviewed periodically to ensure that they are kept to a minimum.

6.9 Forwarding Email:​

Forwarding of business-related email from the Entegris corporate email environment to any personal email accounts for the purpose of bypassing the retention policy is strictly prohibited.

6.10 Enforcement:​

Any user found to have violated this policy may be subject to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.
