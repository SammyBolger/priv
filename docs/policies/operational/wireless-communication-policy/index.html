Wireless (Wi-Fi) Communication Policy

1. Purpose​

  • 1.1 With the mass explosion of Smart Phones and Tablets, pervasive wireless connectivity is almost a given at any organization. Insecure wireless configuration can provide an easy open door for malicious threat vectors.
  • 1.2 The purpose of this policy is to secure and protect the information assets owned by Entegris. Entegris provides computer devices, networks, and other electronic information systems to meet day-to-day goals and corporate mission and initiatives. Entegris grants access to these resources as a privilege and must manage them responsibly to maintain the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all information assets.
  • 1.3 Major thefts of data have been initiated by attackers who have gained wireless access to organizations from outside the physical building, bypassing organizations' security perimeters by connecting wirelessly to access points inside the organization.
  • 1.4 Wireless clients accompanying traveling officials are infected on a regular basis through remote exploitation during air travel or in cyber cafes. Such exploited systems are then used as back doors when they are reconnected to the network of a target organization. Many organizations have reported the discovery of unauthorized wireless access points on their networks, planted and sometimes hidden for unrestricted access to an internal network. As they don’t require direct physical connections, wireless devices are a convenient vector for attackers to maintain long-term access into a target environment.

2. Scope​

  • 2.1 All employees, contractors, consultants, temporary and other workers at Entegris, including all personnel affiliated with third parties that maintain a wireless infrastructure device on behalf of Entegris must adhere to this policy.
  • 2.2 This policy applies to all wireless infrastructure devices that connect to the Entegris network or reside on an Entegris site that provide wireless connectivity to endpoint devices including, but not limited to, laptops, desktops, cellular phones, and tablets.

3. Responsibilities​

  • 3.1 Security of Wi-Fi access points is the responsibility of the corporate IT group. No users should tamper with deployed access points in all office and warehouse locations.

4. References​

  • Reference Documents: 13825 - Wireless Communication Standard
  • Special Notes: Policy # BAI03.05.502

5. Policy​

5.1 General Requirements​

  • 5.1.1 All wireless infrastructure devices that reside at Entegris sites and connect to an Entegris network, or provide access to information classified as that of Entegris must:
    • 5.1.1.1 Abide by the standards specified in the Wireless Communication Standard.
    • 5.1.1.2 Be installed, supported, and maintained by the User or the Entegris Support team.
    • 5.1.1.3 Use Entegris approved authentication protocols and infrastructure.
    • 5.1.1.4 Use Entegris approved encryption protocols.
    • 5.1.1.5 Maintain a hardware address (MAC address) that can be registered and tracked.
    • 5.1.1.6 Not deploy any wireless broadcast devices, such as wireless hubs or routers.

5.2 Lab and Isolated Wireless Device Requirements​

  • 5.2.1 All lab wireless infrastructure devices must restrict access to only the server and ports needed by the application.
