Architecture Principles Defined

Principles serve as a guide and provide general purpose guidance to drive Capability and Solution decisions. Principles provide the guideposts for business and technology leaders to make judgements toward their investment decisions.

Anatomy of a Principle​

Principles should optimize on the best outcomes through:

Simple​

  • Simple statements of value and belief

Forward Looking​

  • Begin with the end in mind working to build principles that are enduring

Anchored​

  • Principles should reflect and reinforce the value chain

Aspirational​

  • Given the journey has just started, we need to set stretch goals

Principles Template​

When defining a principle, use the following structure:

Statement​

  • Statement of the principle/ definition

Rationale​

  • Provide the rationale for the principle

Implications​

  • Describe the implications of the principle including both pros and cons
