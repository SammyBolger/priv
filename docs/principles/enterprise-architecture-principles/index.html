Enterprise Architecture Principles

Design for Customer Centricity​

Principle Name: Design for Customer Centricity

Statement: Focus on customer and user needs within the context of their value chain. Architecture and solution decisions should prioritize customer outcomes and value creation.

Rationale: Customer-centered design ensures investments create meaningful business value and competitive differentiation.

Implications:

  • Customer journeys and Jobs To Be Done should be considered in solution design.
  • Design reviews should include customer perspective and validation.
  • Technology decisions should be evaluated against customer use cases and outcomes.

Solutions Are Democratized​

Principle Name: Solutions Are Democratized

Statement: Technology solutions and data access should be provided through self-serve models that enable teams to operate independently and innovate faster.

Rationale: Self-serve access reduces bottlenecks, improves agility, and supports local decision-making across a distributed enterprise.

Implications:

  • Platforms should provide simple self-service interfaces where possible.
  • Governance should rely on guardrails rather than excessive manual approval.
  • Documentation and support should enable independent use without requiring specialist intervention.

Remove Friction​

Principle Name: Remove Friction

Statement: Solutions should be designed to eliminate unnecessary complexity, delays, and obstacles that impede productivity and adoption.

Rationale: Friction reduces adoption, slows delivery, and increases waste. Every unnecessary step in a process or integration is a cost with no corresponding value.

Implications:

  • User experience and process flow should be reviewed for avoidable friction at every design stage.
  • Integration points should be seamless and low-touch.
  • Teams should actively reduce technology and process debt that creates friction over time.

Demonstrate Trustworthiness and Stewardship​

Principle Name: Demonstrate Trustworthiness and Stewardship

Statement: All solutions and data handling must demonstrate trustworthiness through confidentiality, integrity, and availability in line with sensitivity and context.

Rationale: Trust is essential to customer confidence, employee confidence, and responsible stewardship of data and systems. Entegris handles sensitive material science and customer data that requires a high standard of care.

Implications:

  • Security and privacy should be built into solutions from inception, not added after.
  • Data handling should be transparent and auditable.
  • Compliance expectations should be explicit and enforced across all solution tiers.

Think Big and Execute Rapidly​

Principle Name: Think Big and Execute Rapidly

Statement: Adopt ambitious goals, then pursue them through rapid iteration, learning, and adaptation.

Rationale: Competitive advantage in the semiconductor materials industry requires both strategic vision and execution speed. Neither alone is sufficient.

Implications:

  • Delivery should favor short cycles and feedback loops over long waterfall timelines.
  • Decision-making should be close to execution where practical.
  • Teams should learn quickly and adjust without excessive delay or committee overhead.

Keep It Simple​

Principle Name: Keep It Simple

Statement: Simplicity is paramount in all architecture decisions. Complexity should be avoided unless it is clearly justified by business need.

Rationale: Simple systems are easier to understand, maintain, adopt, and evolve. Complexity compounds over time and creates hidden cost and risk.

Implications:

  • Architecture reviews should explicitly evaluate simplicity as a design criterion.
  • Solutions should avoid unnecessary customization and feature bloat.
  • Maintainability should take precedence over unnecessary sophistication.

Loosely Coupled and Interoperable Solutions​

Principle Name: Loosely Coupled and Interoperable Solutions

Statement: Solutions should be designed with loose coupling, clear interfaces, and interoperability standards so they can evolve independently and integrate cleanly.

Rationale: Loose coupling reduces brittleness and enables change without widespread rework. Tightly coupled systems create hidden dependencies that make change expensive and risky.

Implications:

  • Interfaces should be documented, stable, and versioned.
  • Shared standards should be used for integration and data exchange.
  • Synchronous dependencies should be minimized where practical; asynchronous patterns preferred for resilience.

Flexible Consumption-Based Solutions​

Principle Name: Flexible Consumption-Based Solutions

Statement: Technology solutions should support flexible consumption models so usage can scale with demand and cost follows value.

Rationale: Flexible consumption improves financial transparency, business agility, and the ability to right-size investments as demand evolves.

Implications:

  • Cost models should support consumption visibility and chargeback where appropriate.
  • Vendor and platform choices should preserve commercial flexibility.
  • Capacity planning should accommodate changing demand without over-provisioning.
